What I built
The palette and the rail are one conversation with two viewports, and the Chat tab is its archive. ⌘K is the only way in — typing a question instead of a search term hands the same input to Enola, so there is no separate "open chat" gesture to learn. If the answer wants to sit next to data you are reading, press Chat rail and the same thread docks at 320px with the current screen as its scope. Per-tab chats exist in exactly one place, Academics, because a course's materials are a genuinely closed corpus and citations only mean something inside it.
The rejected option is the persistent rail. On a dense dashboard it costs a third of the width permanently to hold a thread you use in bursts, and it quietly trains you to talk to the app instead of reading it. Enola is mostly something you read.
